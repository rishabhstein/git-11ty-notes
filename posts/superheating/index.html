Superheating

Posted 20 Sep 2025 Modified 10 Nov 2025, 19:01

An interesting phenomenon. I knew the theory from thermodynamics class but a recent post on reddit caught my attention for practical reasons.

Can we superheat the water without observing a boil?

Apparently, yes. If there is no nucleation site, such as in the case of glass container, the water might not produce bubbles which is expected in normal boiling. Further heating can bring it to superheated state, resulting a high pressure blast in the glass container.
