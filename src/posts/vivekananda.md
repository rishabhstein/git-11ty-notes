A famous student of **Rama Krishna Paramhansa**. I have read two books
of him, one is his *memoirs* and second is about *Yoga* which is
classified in four categories:

-   [Karma Yoga](./karmayoga/)
-   [Bhakti Yoga](./bhaktiyoga/)
-   [Raj Yoga](/rajyoga/)
-   [Jnana Yoga](/jnanayoga/)

Recommended:

-   Autobiography, *Swami Vivekananda on Himself*
-   A collection of his lectures on different Yoga philosophy. *The
    Complete Book of Yoga: Karma Yoga, Bhakti Yoga, Raja Yoga, Jnana
    Yoga*
