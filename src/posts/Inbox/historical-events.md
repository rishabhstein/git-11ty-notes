## Latin America:
#### Banana Massacre
It is a real incident which happened in Columbia. Historic details can be found on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banana_Massacre). 

I came to know about this from *Gabriel García Márquez’s* book and TV series, [One hundred years of solitude](https://blog.rishabhps.com/books/2026-09-28-one-hundred-years-of-solitude/). The main reason of this masaccre was the conflict between workers and United Food Company, where workers were demanding better toilets, Sundays off, and 4-5 other things. Although it’s fictional in the book, the details are quite accurate except the number of casualties. Marquez told 3000 while real numbers were between 47-2000.
