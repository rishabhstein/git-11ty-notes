Peclet number (Pe)(\text{Pe})(Pe), named after Jean Claude Eugene Peclet (1793-1857), is a dimensionless number which is very useful in studying different transport phenomena. It is defined as the ratio of diffusive time scale (τD)(\tau_D)(τD​) to advective time scale (τA\tau_AτA​).
