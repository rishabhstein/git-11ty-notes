I am glad you checked this one because it is a Greek word whose meaning you might already know. In Greek, Padeia refers to "knowledge" and Enkuklo is Encyclo in modern English, which means "Circling around". So it is Greek form of the word "Encyclopadeia" which means "Circling around the knowledge"
