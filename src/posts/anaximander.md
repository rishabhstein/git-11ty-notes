He lived in Miletus around the same time as
[Thales](./thalesofmiletus/) and [Anaximenes](./anaximenes/). His
belief was that *our world is one of the myriads of the worlds that
evolve and dissolve in boundless*\[1\]. However, it is not clear what he
meant from boundless. Perhaps, something fundamental of which the world
is made of, in which before and after the existence the world dissolves
into.
