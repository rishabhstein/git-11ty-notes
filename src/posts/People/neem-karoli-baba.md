One of the most revered guru (spiritual teacher) of [[Philosophy/yoga|Bhakti Yoga]] from northern India in last century. He considered himself (and referred himself many times to his devotees) an avatar of Hindu god, *Hanuman*, who is known for his devotion to *Lord Ram*. His primary teachings were based on *Adwait (Non-duality)* that all is part of one consiousness, [[Philosophy/religion|The God]].

He was a popularity magnet (unwillingly) due to his miracles and comments on future events. So to avoid disturbance in his [[Philosophy/religion|spiritual practices]] he moved place to place and lived with different names. He showed his frustation about his popularity many times.

In west he became popular after [[People/ramdas|Ram Dass]] wrote a book about him,[@RamDass1971Beherenow] where he shared his own experiences of *Neem Karoli Baba*. After that many famous people, including *Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg and Yuval Noah Harrari* claimed to have come to India to meet Baba. *Krishna Das* is another student of baba who is known for his songs devoted to hindu Gods.

Recently a movie, *Hanuman Ansh*, was released in Bollywood as a first part of triology based on baba’s life.
