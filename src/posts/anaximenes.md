He was from Miletus, same as [Thales](./thalesofmiletus/) and
[Anaximander](./anaximander/), and they all might have existed at the
same time. Anaximenes believed that the **source of all things is *air*
or *vapor*** \[1\]. Water exists in air untill pressed out. At higher
pressure it convert to water and then later to ice.
