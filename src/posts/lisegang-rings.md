In diffusion dominated environment where reactant A diffuses in reactant B and forms C, concentric rings made of C appears with increasing spacing while going far from injection point.

![Lisegang rings ](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b0/Liesegang_rings.jpg)


The common explanation, according to [Boeke's theory](boeke-theory.md), is that the reaction front between A and B moves due to diffusion and constantly forms C. When the concentration of C surpasses certain threshold it precipitates, forming a ring. However, in this duration, concentration of A and B drops, resulting a decrease in concentration of C, and therefore precipitation stops for certain duration, after which a second precipitation event comes again, following the same process. This duration of no precipitation region increases as the front moves further away.
