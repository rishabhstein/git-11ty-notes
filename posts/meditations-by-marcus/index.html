Meditations: Marcus Aurelius
Born26 April 121, Rome
Died17 March 180 (age 58)
SchoolStoicism
Known forFirst Philosopher King
StudentsAntoninus

Around 2000 years old journal of one of the most powerful king of Roman empire. Full of inspiring entries (Quotes). There are a few which, ironically, still are so applicable. A few, I like:

  1. You could leave right now...
    Let that determine what you do and say and think.

    ---Book Two, P.20

  2. No random actions...
    None not based on underlying principles.

    ---Book Four, P.01
