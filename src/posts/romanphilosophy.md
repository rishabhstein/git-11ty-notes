A notebook to find the ~~Roman~~ philosophers at one place. The western philosophers can be categorised in two groups:
#### Pre-socratic and Sophists

-   [Thales](thalesofmiletus)
-   [Anaximander](anaximander)
-   [Anaximenes](anaximenes)
-   [Parmenides](parmenides)
-   [Plato](plato)

#### Socratic

-   [Socrates](socrates)
-   [Aristotle](aristotle)
-   [Democritus](democritus)


The key distinction in these two categories is the existence of *Socrates*. Before him, the attempt to understand the world was purely based on speculations and observations. Pre-socratic philosophers and Sophists were dogmatic, proposing hypothesis without testing them. In another words they were not scientists.

Arrival of *Socrates*, and later [Aristotle](aristotle), changed the game completely. Now the question of *Why?* became important. Mere speculations was not acceptable and consideration of *self-inquiry* became important.
