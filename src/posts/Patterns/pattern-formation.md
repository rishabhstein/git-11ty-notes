## Definition
Pattern formation is a fascinating phenomenon where patterns emerge in seemingly chaotic systems. Patterns can be mathematical, such as an algebraic series, but I am referring here patterns in physical, chemical, or biological systems observed in nature or laboratory.

Patterns are as old as nature but they were recognised and appreciated first by human beings. Spirals drawn by prehistoric humans in cave paintings [Citation] indicates that they knew about patterns. The first documented study on recording patterns was done by [[People/leonaro-vinci|Leonardo Da Vinci]].

Then comes Darcy Thompson.
