#### Carbon and energy first: Hydrothermal vents and the origin of microbial metabolism

Talk of William F. Gardin from University of Düsseldorf, Germany.
- Life arose in hydrothermal vent in ocean
- **John Baross** (Uni of Washington) first proposed in 1981 and 1985 paper about this hypothesis
- Kelley et al., Nature 30–The lost city in ocean
- Two life forms live on $H_2$ (Acetogens Bacteria) and $CO_2$ (Archaea)
- Chemical self-organization leads to origin of life.
