Some comments for the critism on PhD thesis writing on Academia Stack
Exchange
[\[Link\]](https://academia.stackexchange.com/questions/145912/how-to-to-deal-with-advisors-criticism-of-my-writing)

-   It would be a mistake to interpret **criticism** as *assertion that
    you\'re failing*. Rather, [it is a constructive response to the work
    you\'ve done so far.]{.underline}
-   Ultimately, [you may never produce anything that makes your advisor
    completely happy]{.underline}. You are different people. The
    objective is to produce clear, readable papers, not to mimic
    perfectly your advisor\'s style.
-   I suggest dealing with his comments in two phases. During the first
    phase, look for comments that are about what you are saying, and
    firmly ignore any comments about how you are saying it.
-   **Revisions?** [It\'s not oh, you are
    wrong/bad/evil/stupid]{.underline}, but more like *you can do better
    at this point* and better serve yourself, make a better impression,
    be more successful with other people.
