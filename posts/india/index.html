India (history)

Posted 19 Jan 2025 Modified 15 Oct 2025, 19:48

Contents

  2. Recommended

It is rather a complicated topic as majority of the history of common knowledge was documented and reported from post-colonial era. Many argue about this history including Dharampal who claimed in his book The Beautiful Tree that there is a falsification of data about Indian history reported by British and other European historians. Controversial!!. However, for me, the most fascinating is the history of India in pre-colonial era.

William Dalrymple in his recent book pointed a very intriguing idea of "golden road which was a sea route actually". It existed even before the famous silk road, and vanished after the fall of Roman empire and the rise of Mongol empire.

Alexander Frater in his travel book Chasing the monsoon, while travelling through Kerala, observed that it had as diverse population as Dalrymple described it in Buddhist era, around 2000 years ago:

..the first time, I was struck by the notion that I was just one alien among many. This seemed to be a nation of millions of foreigners, a bewildering accretion of mutually exclusive tongues, gods and cultures the governance of which, shaky although it might be, appeared nothing sort of miraculous. (Ch. 2)

Recommended:

  • William Dalrymple, The Golden Road: How Ancient India Transformed the World
  • Alexander Frater Chasing the monsoon
