The idea of a fundamental particle (maybe unit) seems to be originated in Greek and Indian philosophy independently. In Greek philosophy, [Democritus](democritus.md) suggested the idea of atom around 400-300 BCE, while in [Indian Philosophy](indianphilosophy.md), It was [Kanada](kanada.md) who proposed the idea of paramanu (Atom) around 600-200 BCE.

However, both ideas of atom are very different. [Democritus's](democritus.md) atom has no qualitative differences, while [Kanada's](kanada.md) atom differed both quantitatively and qualitatively.
