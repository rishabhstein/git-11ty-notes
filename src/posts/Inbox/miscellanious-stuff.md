## Commonplace book
Its different than journaling or writing a diary. The main idea is to keep things you like at one place, such as a recipe, quote from an author and a scientific concept. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonplace_book) is a good place to look for details.

I realized that this website itself follow the idea of commonplace book. Interestingly, [[Seneca]] and [[Cicero]] also recommend it in their writings.

## Meme
A concept proposed by [[People/Richard-Dawkins]] in his book [[Selfish-gene]] explaining the spread of an contagious idea, such as religion. The idea is similar to a virus, however a psychological one.
