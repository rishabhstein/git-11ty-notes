- Observed in plants such as sunflower and pine cones
- Commonly referred through phyllotaxy phenomenon where logarithmic spiral following *Fibonacci* series appear.
- The number of total spirals is $(n+m)$ where $n$ is number of spiral in one direction and $m$ is in another.
- Interestingly, $n$ and $m$ are always (hopefully?) , two consecutive number of *Fibonacci* series.[@dewitChemohydrodynamicPatternsPorous2016; @facchiniPhyllotacticStructuresRadially2025; @santiago-rosanneDissolutionDropLiquid1997; @douadyPhyllotaxisPhysicalSelforganized1992]
- There are many theories for appearance of phyllotaxy in different systems[@kornPhyllotaxisTheoriesEvaluation]
