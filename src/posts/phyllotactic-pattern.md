- Observed in plants such as sunflower and pine cones
- Commonly referred through phyllotaxy phenomenon where logarithmic spiral following *Fibonacci* series appear.
- The number of total spirals is $(n+m)$ where $n$ is number of spiral in one direction and $m$ is in another.
- Interestingly, $n$ and $m$ are always (hopefully?) , two consecutive number of *Fibonacci* series.[^1][^2][^3][^4]
- There are many theories for appearance of phyllotaxy in different systems[^5]
	- **Available space theory**: New leaf forms in the largest available space which is usually located opposite to the last one or two that formed.

	- **Biophysical theory:** Darwin himself
	- **Vascular theory**
