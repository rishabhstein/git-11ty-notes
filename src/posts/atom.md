The idea of a fundamental particle (maybe unit) seems to be originated
in Greek and Indian philosophy independently. In Greek philosophy,
[Democritus](./democritus/) suggested the idea of atom around 400-300
BCE, while in [Indian philosophy](./indianPhilosophy/), It was
[Kanada](./kanada/) who proposed the idea of paramanu (Atom) around
600-200 BCE.\
\
However, both ideas of atom are very different.
[Democritus's](./democritus/) atom has no qualitative differences,
while [Kanada's](./kanada/) atom differed both quantitatively and
qualitatively.
