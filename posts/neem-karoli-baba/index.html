Neem Karoli Baba

Posted 22 Oct 2026 Modified 22 Oct 2026, 16:01

Contents
  1. (Top)
  2. See Also

  4. Recommend

One of the most revered guru (spiritual teacher) of Bhakti Yoga from northern India in last century. He considered himself (and referred himself many times to his devotees) an avatar of Hindu god, Hanuman, who is known for his devotion to Lord Ram. His primary teachings were based on Adwait (Non-duality) that all is part of one consiousness, The God.

He was a popularity magnet (unwillingly) due to his miracles and comments on future events. So to avoid disturbance in his spiritual practices he moved place to place and lived with different names. He showed his frustation about his popularity many times.

In west he became popular after Ram Dass wrote a book about him,[1] where he shared his own experiences of Neem Karoli Baba. After that many famous people, including Steve Jobs, Mark Zuckerberg and Yuval Noah Harrari claimed to have come to India to meet Baba. Krishna Das is another student of baba who is known for his songs devoted to hindu Gods.

Recently a movie, Hanuman Ansh, was released in Bollywood as a first part of triology based on baba’s life.
