*What is philosophy and what is it useful for?* The answer to this
question changed with time. In old times, it was a guide to live an
ideal life \[1\], which is also evident in [Indian
philosophies](./indianphilosophy/) as well as in Roman philosophies
such as [Stoicism](./stoicism/) and **Cynicism**. Nevertheless, in
modern times, it became a medium of intellectual pleasure, resulting in
the limited usage of it in daily life.

Philosophy is a way of life and not simply a theoretical discipline. 
  
**--- Epictetus**
