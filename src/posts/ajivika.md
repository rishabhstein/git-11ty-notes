Ajivika or Ajivikism--as west pronounces it--is an Indian philosophy of [Nastika](nastika/) founded by some guy **Goshala** who believed in **Niyati** or **Fatalism**. I found this reference in a [book](book/), The Discovery of India, by Nehru but Wiki says the sources can be found in [Buddhism](buddhism) and [Jainism](jainism).
